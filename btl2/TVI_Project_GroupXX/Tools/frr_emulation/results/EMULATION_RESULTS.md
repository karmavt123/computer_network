# FRR emulation of the TVI routing design - results

Generated by `Tools/frr_emulation/emulate.py` on 2026-11-14 15:55 (FRR 8.4.4, Linux namespaces).

Link model: every link passes through a bridge (as in EVE-NG/GNS3) - a failed interface or a dead router leaves the far end up/up, so neighbours must detect the failure with their protocol timers (OSPF dead 4 s, HSRP hold 1.5 s, BGP hold 9 s). This is the conservative case.

> **What this is:** the IPv4 routing sections of the generated Cisco startup-configs, translated mechanically
> (`ios2frr.py`) and executed by real OSPF/BGP daemons. **What it is not:** the graded EVE-NG/IOSv lab.
> IPsec is replaced by VXLAN tunnels, HSRP by a rule engine applying the configured priority/track/preempt
> values (hold time and track polling interval honoured, preempt delay scaled x0.1); L2/ACL/ZBF/QoS/IPv6 are not emulated.
> Outage figures are for this emulation only - measure your own in the lab.
